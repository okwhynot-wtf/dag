## 1. The primitive is an act

Reality originates from an **act**, not from a static entity.

The primitive element is not matter, energy, space, information, or consciousness.

It is the simplest possible act: **the act of distinction**.

---

## 2. No privileged side

A fundamental distinction cannot privilege either side.

If one side were intrinsically preferred, the explanation would already presuppose structure.

Therefore the primitive action must exhibit **perfect symmetry**.

---

## 3. Only one survivor

Classify every possible self-action. Reject those that:

- collapse distinctions,
- become stationary,
- smuggle in direction, or
- require additional structure.

Exactly one survives:

> **Boolean complement** — the cut.

*(In the formal spine this is the unique Fundamental: negation on `Bool`.)*

---

## 4. The foundation is dynamic

The fundamental law has **no fixed point**.

Reality is not a static being. Reality is **perpetual distinction**.

---

## 5. Self-reference leaves a blind spot

Whenever a system attempts a complete description of itself, there always remains a valid description external to that account.

This is the familiar pressure of self-reference and diagonalisation.

The universe is therefore **perpetually incomplete from within**.

---

## 6. Growth is necessary

The only way to accommodate the missing description is to enlarge the arena.

```text
distinction
    → diagonalisation
    → new possibilities
    → a larger conceptual arena
    → (repeat indefinitely)
```

Growth is not an add-on. It is forced by incompleteness.

---

## 7. Records and archives

Records appear when distinctions are **preserved**.

If evolution never destroys distinctions, then apparent forgetting must be accompanied by hidden bookkeeping.

**Loss implies archives.**

---

## 8. Time as reading

Time is the process of **reading the archive**.

The archive itself has no intrinsic direction. Observers define direction from their position within it.

The arrow of time is therefore not a fundamental property. It is an **orientation on accumulated records**.

---

## 9. The subject is not an object

The most fundamental identity does not correspond to any of the distinguished states.

It is the act of distinction itself.

> The true subject is identical with the lawful cut — not with any object it distinguishes.

---

## 10. Physics is downstream

Matter, entropy, memory, measurement, observers, and spacetime are interpretations of the bookkeeping generated by the preceding principles.

None of these elements constitute the foundation. Physics functions as a **downstream interpretive dictionary**.

*(In the hybrid architecture: Spine → Ledger → Dictionary.)*

---

## Conclusion

The universe is not composed of discrete things.

It consists of distinctions perpetually engaged in self-differentiation.
